::LOG.1:: - July 20, 2026

We got to Ben-Gurion at 3:50 israel time, after an incredibly painful flight on a 787, in which:

  • I had to endure 8 hours of butt pain
  • I dropped my dirty fork and spoon into my neighbors seat (or nearly did at least)
  • I shot water all over my stuff after opening my bottle at 30k feet
  • I gave a flight attendant my pillowcase to throw away after thinking it was just a disposable wrapper
  • I couldn’t charge my phone and use it because there were only USB-A ports, so my only source of entertainment was the screen on the back of the seat.

I couldn’t watch anything because it didn’t have bluetooth audio, and I had no wired headphones.1 So I stared at the flight tracking thing most of the time, and the rest of the time I played “Checkmate Chess.” For some reason they couldn’t just call it “Chess.” Although, maybe it was called that because it had a single difficulty, which seemed to be a 1500+ ELO bot. Zero options to play against someone else on the plane or anything, just a single gamemode in which 90% of casual players zero chance of winning. Well played, Ensemble Media (that’s the developer of Checkmate Chess). Although maybe it was actually an adaptive bot. It sure didn’t go easy on me, though - the most I could do was draw against it twice.

Anyway, Ben Gurion was full of asian tourists. I tried asking two of them where they were from, and I was met with silence and cold, blank, stares. Maybe not cold, just confused. It was funny. Then (actually before this) I went through the weird Israeli passport control. They give you a ticket and then summarily dismiss you with one english word: “BYE.” Shit was downright hilarious. After a head count of sorts in the baggage claim, we left the airport and ate some extremely soggy rugelach type things. It was HOT. Then we got on the bus to go to Mount Scopus to do the culty thing I’ve ever done: Rending a garment at the sight of the temple mount. Peak self awareness.2

We davened mincha there, and then got in the buses again, for the western wall this time. It was fucking FULL of girls. Like hot damn they were everywhere, and just doing random B.S for no reason. Seeing the western wall was a cool experience and all; we davened maariv in a weird disjointed minyan and then came to the dorms of this yeshiva that I don’t even know the name of. The dinner was mid and the speeches were mid-er. The atmosphere of this place is really irritating.

Random event of the day: Rabbi complained to me about the gentrification of Jerusalem; I then taught him the word gentrification.

::LOG.2:: - July 21, 2026

I don’t know the time because my phone seems to be broken. I woke up around 8:10 AM, and recited morning prayers. Ate breakfast, then took a dump and missed role call at the 10AM-12:30PM seder, which I spent most of doodling on the sheet I was given until I was volun-told into actually getting a chavrusa. Once that nightmare was over, we went back to the temple mount, but to the archeological parts this time. We saw the famous pile of pushed/fallen stones, Mohammed’s “pinky hole” or whatever the fuck, and Al-Aqsa mosque from below. We went through the old city, all of the gates, and the rothschild building (which was basically a homeless shelter built by Edmund Rothschild, and it’s now a weird school). After sitting around there, we all went into thi dug-out area with roman pillars and we had one of those back-killing fuckass kumzitzes. I think we finally left for Bnei Brak after that. The drive was long and boring, I fell asleep approximately 10 times. After finally getting there, we put on our hats and jackets and thus commenced the extreme suffering.

We went to two places, and I nearly died of heatstroke at the second one. The neighborhood was extremely dirty, and every single building was covered in AC units and cables and railings. 3 It looks simultaneously cool and terrible. Once that was over with, we pulled up at a random park and trashed the place in the process of eating pizza for dinner. There was a cemetary across the street where the chazon ish and some other randos are buried. It was full of trash and cramped. I went to the bus and waited for ten years for everyone to come back, and then after sleeping the entire ride back I missed the opportunity to go out shopping (on foot) because I went to the bathroom. What a tragically poetic ending to the day.
