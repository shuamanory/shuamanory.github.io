I've been working on yt-dlp_GUI on and off for around two years now. I've finally made the decision to stop work on it, and leave it in a non-working state. Youtube alters their APIs constantly; it's too much of an uphill battle to constantly make tweaks to it and at the same time ensure that it's not just "working on my machine." 

There was a period of time when it really was the best solution for me personally, which made working on it worth it. However, I no longer really have a need for it. Not only do perfectly sufficient replacements exist, I just don't need to save youtube videos anymore. I use SoulSeek now[^1]...

Disregarding whatever I just said, that should explain why I'm killing it. RIP, but it fulfilled its purpose in life, so.. no regrets.

[^1]: For legal reasons, this is entirely facetious
